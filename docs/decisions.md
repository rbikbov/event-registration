- **22:38** — an email (of the organizer and of a participant) is normalized only by case; dots and
  `+tag` in the local part are significant. Why: by RFC 5321 (§2.3.11) the meaning of the local part

  and in Google Workspace and with other providers dots are significant — removing them would merge
  different people on corporate domains. A caveat: RFC 5321 (§2.4) requires treating the local part

  distinguish case, and otherwise `Ivan@` and `ivan@` would take two seats. The consequence: the
  owner of one Gmail mailbox can take several seats with different spellings of the address — we
  protect against an accidental duplicate, not against a deliberate workaround. Postponed (if it
  becomes important): a canonical form for specific providers or storing the original and the
  canonical address. Rejected: removing dots from all addresses — it contradicts the RFC.
